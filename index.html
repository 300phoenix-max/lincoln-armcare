HOUSE RULES

  • Never throw with a sore or tired arm. Pain in the elbow or shoulder = automatic rest until it's 100% gone.
  • Age 10 limit: 75 pitches max per day. Rest days after pitching: 21–35 = 1 day · 36–50 = 2 · 51–65 = 3 · 66+ = 4.
  • All throwing counts: games, lessons, bullpens, backyard.
  • Bands stay light — smooth and slow, never straining.
  • 2–3 months per year with zero overhead throwing.
  • No pitching and catching in the same day.
